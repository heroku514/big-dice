Big Dice puts huge dice and a score table on the phone you pass around the table. Kids can tap Roll. Grandparents can read the numbers from across the room. Families can keep four scores without a pencil.

Choose one, two, or three dice. Roll, see the total, and add it to the person whose turn it is. Names stay on this phone, and so do the scores.

Big Dice does not use an account, ads, or a network connection. It is a table tool for a board game you already own.
